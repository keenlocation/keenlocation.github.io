Historical replay uses closed Binance daily candles. A simulated contribution buys at the daily close with the chosen fee. Portfolio values exclude exit costs. The lump-sum comparison invests the final total budget at the first historical close.

The AI bot name is a project theme. Calculations are transparent rules and simulations, with no trained AI model or trade execution. Fees are editable assumptions.
